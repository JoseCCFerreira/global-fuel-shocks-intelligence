# Data Sources

## Fuel Prices

World Bank Commodity Markets Pink Sheet provides long-run monthly commodity prices including crude oil, natural gas, coal and energy indices.

## Retail Pump Prices

World Bank Indicators API includes selected country-level gasoline and diesel pump price indicators, typically annual and sparse.

## Natural Disasters

Our World in Data / EM-DAT provides disaster-related indicators such as economic damage. Coverage and reporting quality vary over time and by country.

## Wars and Conflicts

Our World in Data / UCDP provides armed-conflict death estimates by country/year. These are event severity proxies, not full geopolitical causal models.

## House Prices

BIS residential property price data, or public mirror datasets, can be used to compare housing-market variation with fuel-price cycles.

## Population

World Bank `SP.POP.TOTL` provides country and world population totals.
